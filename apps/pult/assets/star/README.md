# The Star's stills

Rendered by `../pult-star` from its Astra sketch, never edited here: `pnpm stills stills/astra.json` there writes `out/stills/astra/`, laid out as this directory, which takes it as it is (its `settings.json` left out). `stills/astra.json` holds the settings Anton picked on its generated page (surface, seed, size, the moment of the flow, every state's look).

`<tint>/<state>.png` is the Star's light on transparency, for dark themes; `<tint>/<state>.ink.png` its ink, for light ones. 64 px a side, shown at 22 px on the card. The states are xaxis `pult/issues/star-states.md`, the tints `TINTS` in `src/stars/record.ts`.
