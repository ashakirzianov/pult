# Edits to the copy

This directory is the fork's `apps/web` at the commit in `UPSTREAM`, as far as the view's files of ours (`../shell/`) reach it; `pnpm vendor:merge` keeps it so (`pult/decisions`, `thread-view-source`). What the shell's thread pane needs from the app around the view lives in files of ours: `../shell/` mounts upstream's components, and `../stubs.ts` replaces whole upstream modules. Editing a copied file is the exception, made when neither can do it; each edited file is listed here, one bullet with its reason (`` - `src/a.ts`: why ``), and the merge carries it through upstream's changes. `pnpm vendor:check` fails on a copied file that differs from upstream at `UPSTREAM` and is not listed, and on a listed file that does not differ.

- `src/components/chat/ComposerFooterControls.tsx`: `ComposerControlsSlot`, a context the app around the view may fill with an element, and `ComposerControlsSlotItem`, which portals an item there; with a slot, the context window meter renders in it. Its item carries `data-level`, `heavy` from 150K tokens in context and `full` from 75% of the window (`contextLevel`), which the shell's bar draws orange and red, ahead of upstream's red past 90%. Pult's shell puts the slot's items in its thread pane's composer bar (`../shell/ThreadView.tsx`); without a slot the composer is upstream's.
- `src/components/chat/ChatComposer.tsx`: with a `ComposerControlsSlot`, the footer's settings (`ComposerControlsBar`: model, traits, mode) render in the slot, in their expanded form while the composer rests, and so do its attach and send buttons, leaving the composer its message alone; the slot, the app's element, sits at the end of the composer's form while it is mounted (`../shell/ComposerBar.tsx`'s `ComposerBarHost`), so its buttons submit the form; the notices, the sync row and the task progress render in the slot too, as entries of ours (`../shell/ComposerBar.tsx`), not above the composer.
- `src/components/ChatView.tsx`: with a `ComposerControlsSlot`, the draft's environment and branch strip renders in the slot, the resting model strip does not mount, and the composer's shell leaves no seam for either; and a timeline following its end stays there as the composer's reserved room grows, since the shell's composer is opaque. A background thread's view (`BackgroundThreadContext`, the shell's composer under a file) leaves paste-to-focus to the pane's view, as it does the page-wide shortcuts.
- `src/hooks/useTheme.ts`: `setSystemColorSchemeSource`, through which the app around the view hands the system's colour scheme in place of the browser's `prefers-color-scheme`. Pult's shell hands the workbench theme's (`../shell/main.tsx`), leaving the page's `window.matchMedia` the browser's; without one the view reads the browser's, as upstream's does.
- `src/components/chat/ComposerCitationBlock.tsx`: ours, not upstream's. The composer's assistant citation, and its file or diff comment (upstream's review comment, but for a pull request's; its comment the record's text, its quote the path, the range and the lines, a fresh one Pult cited taking focus in the composer in sight, `requestReviewCommentFocus`), as a block on its own line, the quote and then the comment, edited in place in the node view's own field, the comment still the node's attribute (`pult/issues/composer-citation-blocks`): Backspace or Left (Up from the line below) into a block enters its comment at the end, Delete or Right (Down from the line above) at the start; the field leaves by Left or Up at its start, Right or Down at its end, Escape and Tab, for the neighbouring block's comment where no text stands between, the caret not showing beside a block; Shift+arrows (with Option or without) next to a block extend the selection over the block alone; Enter leaves onto an empty line after the block, a new one unless the next line is empty, and Shift+Enter is a new line in the comment; the block takes the accent's bar, and its tint while the comment is not edited; a comment empty when the field loses focus removes the citation, with its line.
- `src/components/ComposerPromptEditorTiptap.tsx`: the citation's node view renders `ComposerCitationBlock` in place of the chip and its comment popover, and the editor's key handler gives the block its keys first; a requested comment focuses the block's field. A context reference to a file or diff comment renders `ComposerReviewCommentBlock` the same way.
- `src/components/chat/ChatComposer.tsx`: a cited selection is inserted on a line of its own, and so are file and diff comments' references placed at the caret; the composer hands the blocks of file and diff comments the draft's `addReviewComment` and `removeReviewComment` (`ComposerReviewCommentEditContext`).
- `src/components/chat/AssistantSelectionToolbar.tsx`: the button reads "Type to cite", and while it shows a typed character cites the selection with that character starting its comment.
- `src/components/chat/composerFocusRedirect.ts`: the selection toolbar counts as a floating layer, so typing while it shows cites the selection rather than going to the composer.
